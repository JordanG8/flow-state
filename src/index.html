How to move

Hold the phone upright, like a window onto the course. Look around by turning it. You run on your own. Four rotations do everything. Slow rotation steers; fast rotation is a move.

Turn

Corners are painted red. Snap the phone about 90 degrees toward the bend. Snap early; it is buffered.

Leap

Flick the top of the phone up. Clears gaps, steps and red hurdles. Rings in the air build flow.

Slide

Flick down. Slides under red bars. In the air it dives you to the floor and into a slide.

Dash

Tilt the phone sideways like a key in a lock. A short sideways burst for zig-zags.

Red kills. White holds you. Time slows when you snap. Fly through rings in order and the music climbs the scale with you. Keyboard: A/D steer, Q/E turn, Space leap, S slide, Z/C dash. Touch: drag steers, swipe moves.
