How to move

Hold the phone however you like: standing, sitting, lying in bed. You run on your own. Four wrist moves do everything. Slow rotation steers; a fast flick is a move. Every flick resets itself, so you never have to turn around.

Turn

Corners are painted red. Snap your wrist toward the bend and let it spring back. Snap early; it is buffered.

Leap

Flick the top of the phone up and back. Clears gaps, steps and red hurdles. Rings in the air build flow.

Slide

Flick down. Slides under red bars. In the air it dives you to the floor and into a slide.

Dash

Tilt the phone sideways like a key in a lock. A short sideways burst for zig-zags.

Red kills. White holds you. Time slows when you snap. Fly through rings in order and the music climbs the scale with you. Keyboard: A/D steer, Q/E turn, W or Space leap, S slide, Z/C dash, V camera. Mouse looks around, left click leaps, right click slides, Esc pauses. Touch: drag steers, swipe moves.
