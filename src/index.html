How to move

Run: you run on your own. On a phone, drag to steer and swipe to move. A swipe is buffered, so make it early.

Turn

Corners are painted red. Swipe left or right as you reach one. Keyboard: Q and E.

Leap

Swipe up. Clears gaps, steps and red hurdles. Rings in the air build flow. Keyboard: Space.

Slide

Swipe down. Slides under red bars. In the air it dives you to the floor and into a slide. Keyboard: S.

Dash

Tap twice on the left or right half of the screen. A short sideways burst for zig-zags. Keyboard: Z and C.

Red kills. White holds you. Time slows when you turn. Fly through rings in order and the music climbs the scale with you. Keyboard: A/D steer, V camera. Mouse looks around, left click leaps, right click slides, Esc pauses.

Arena: left thumb moves, right thumb looks, and the Fire button also drags the view so you can track while shooting. Keyboard: WASD, mouse, click to fire, Space jump, R reload, V camera, Tab scores. Jump at any tall wall while moving along it and you run on it; jump again to kick off. The striped walls are the only way over the drop.
